## 9. Verification instructions

Google chooses which verification options you see. Follow what the dashboard offers:

1. Sign in at https://business.google.com with the Google account you want to own the profile. Use a long-term account, not a personal account you might lose.
2. Click **Add your business** and enter **Nazora Tech**.
3. Select your categories and choose **service-area business** if you do not serve customers at a public address.
4. Enter the phone number and website exactly as shown above.
5. When Google asks to verify, pick the option offered:
   - **Video verification** (common for service-area businesses): record one continuous video showing your location, your business equipment (computer, work setup), and proof you operate the business (for example, your website admin or client invoice, with private details hidden).
   - **Phone or SMS**: enter the code Google sends.
   - **Email**: click the link in the email.
   - **Postcard**: enter the code on the mailed card; it can take several days.
6. Do not request verification repeatedly. Wait for Google's response before trying again.
7. After verification, complete the profile fully: description, services, areas, photos, and hours.
